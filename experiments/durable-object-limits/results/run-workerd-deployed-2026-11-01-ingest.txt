of 1,024 tags (775,168 tag rows) is the largest call committed, and the one
above it exceeded the object's CPU time limit and reset it, where the run of
2026-10-31 committed 1,016 and was reset by the storage-operation timeout. The
wall moved 1.34 times between two runs and is not a function of the tag count
alone. ADR-0100 answers it with a declared ceiling of 524,288 tags per call,
1.48 times below the lower wall, refused before any SQL as
StoreLimit::TagsPerBatch, class=Split. minimal and max-data reach the same
sizes as the earlier run; no D7 class was falsified.
