/ingest-ceiling/<shape>?events=<n> and /ingest-class/<case> routes
(src/ingest/ceiling.rs, src/ingest/classes.rs). Every attempt goes through
CloudflareIngestStore::namespaced_from_object_state over the object's real
DurableObjectState, so each call is one real ctx.storage.transactionSync.
Taken 2026-10-31 on Linux (Node v22.23.4), on
claude/project-thread-uikw3l-108e3's working tree (parent cd99a195).

The ceiling is bisected by the runner (ingest-ceiling.mjs), one object per
attempt, one group of foreign events per call, no compensation. "accepted N"
is the largest call the runtime committed; the refusal one step above it is
quoted verbatim, with its HTTP status. The round trip is the runner's, at N.
Shapes: minimal = 8 B of data and 1 tag per event; max-tags = 8 B and 1,024
distinct tags (the declared MAX_TAGS_PER_EVENT); max-data = 1 MiB of data
(the declared MAX_EVENT_DATA_LEN) and 1 tag. minimal and max-tags are searched
to 1,025, one past the declared 1,024 events per call; max-data to 256.
