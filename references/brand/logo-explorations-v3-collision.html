Round 3 — the brightness-glyph collision

SD-0002 names one unresolved defect: rasterised, the mark reads as the display-brightness glyph every operating system ships. The annotation placement protects the lockup; it does nothing for the standalone mark, which is what appears in the favicon, the crates.io avatar and the GitHub org icon — the surfaces a Rust library is actually seen on. These are the three levers SD-0002 named, cut and judged at the size where it matters.

Breaking the eight-fold symmetry defeats the system-glyph reading — no icon set uses seven — and it feels drawn rather than generated. One block at twelve o'clock puts the gap at six and keeps the mark symmetric about the vertical axis, so the odd count reads as chosen rather than as a piece that fell off. Keeps the sun, which the brief asked for.
Fallback
C · eight tangential
Each record turned 90° to lie around the ring instead of pointing out of it. The brightness reading disappears completely — light radiates, records queue — and it holds at 16px better than anything else here. Rejected on cost: it is no longer a sun. Retained as the fallback if seven proves insufficient.

D · seven tangential
Both levers at once. Tangential blocks need even spacing to read as a sequence; at seven they tile badly and the ring looks mis-set. The two mitigations interfere.

E · eight radial, sharp corners
Predicted in the brand sheet and confirmed: squaring the corners buys nothing against brightness and walks straight back into the gear. The trade was real and it is a bad one.

Tangential element count, for the record

Recorded because C is the standing fallback. Tangential blocks are a rhythm, and the count sets what the rhythm reads as. Too many and it becomes a loading spinner, which is a worse collision than the one being solved.

What this settles, and what it does not

Adopted: B, seven radial. It defeats the eight-fold reading, keeps the sun the brief asked for, and holds its form at the sizes where the mark stands alone. The count is now part of the identity and is recorded as binding in SD-0002.

What is not settled is the evidence. Seven is a reasoned defence that has been looked at by the people who already know what the mark is meant to be, which is the weakest possible test. The check that would settle it is showing the mark at 16px to three Rust developers who have seen neither it nor the record, and asking what they think it is. Until then the collision is mitigated and unfalsified rather than closed.
