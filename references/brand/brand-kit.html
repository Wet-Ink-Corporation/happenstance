Brand Kit · v1 · August 2026

happenstance

Seven equal records radiating from a solid disc. The records are a log — same size, same pitch, no privileged first one. The disc is the boundary drawn across them. Everything in this kit derives from those two shapes.

01The idea

happenstance is a storage-agnostic, DCB-compliant event sourcing library for Rust. Its central idea is that a consistency boundary is drawn by what actually occurred, rather than fixed in advance when a schema is written — deliberate, but not pre-declared.

The mark states that directly. The equal blocks are records in an append-only log: identical, evenly pitched, none of them first. The solid centre is the boundary those records turned out to form. Read together, they say the shape came from the sequence.

The palette carries the second half of the personality. Warm amber against a warm near-black is a deliberate departure from the greys and blues of the surrounding ecosystem, and it signals what the project is: precise, but not cold.

02The mark

The mark is built on a 100-unit square from a single block repeated seven times at a pitch of 360°/7. One block sits at twelve o'clock, which places the gap at six and keeps the mark symmetric about the vertical axis. Every dimension is specified; none is drawn by eye.

03Clear space

Clear space is one block length — 13 units, or 17% of the artwork width — on every side. Nothing sets inside it: no type, no rule, no edge of a container. The measure scales with the mark, so it holds at any size.

04Sizes and variants

The mark ships in two weights. Primary is the standard drawing and is used at 32px and above. Compact is a heavier reduction for 32px and below, with a larger centre and thicker blocks so the form survives rasterisation. The badge places the compact mark on an ink plate, which guarantees contrast on any browser chrome.

05Wordmark

The wordmark is set lowercase, always — matching the crate name, including at the start of a sentence. Where a sentence would force a capital, recast the sentence.

07Colour

Five colours. Sun is the identity and appears on every application. Ink and Paper carry everything else — both are warm, and the warmth is deliberate: it keeps the amber from sitting against a neutral it has nothing in common with.

08Typography

IBM Plex Sans for everything set in words; IBM Plex Mono for code, identifiers, versions and file paths. One family, two voices, open licence — and a mono that was designed alongside the sans rather than found to match it.

09Correct use

The mark is fixed artwork. Place it, scale it, and choose a colourway from the palette — those are the available decisions.

Upright, at any scale
Never rotate
Never stretch or condense
Never recolour outside the palette
RuleDetail
Block count is fixedSeven, always. The count is part of the identity.
Orientation is fixedOne block at twelve o'clock. The mark never rotates.
Proportions are fixedScale uniformly. No stretching, condensing, arching or perspective.
Colour comes from the paletteSun, Ink or Paper as a single fill. No gradients, shadows, glows or outlines.
Clear space is honouredOne block length on every side, at every size.
The wordmark stays lowercaseRecast the sentence rather than capitalise the name.
The mark follows the wordIn the lockup the mark annotates the final e. It is never placed before the word.
