## Questions

- **Is "fails loudly" expressible in the existing error vocabulary?** **Answered, and this story is where the answer becomes an artefact.** No: `read_decision_model` returns `(Vec<SequencedEvent>, Option<SequencePosition>)` with no completeness channel (`crates/happenstance-core/src/store.rs:321-331`); `Guard::is_violated_by` is a two-arm `match` with no third outcome (`crates/happenstance-core/src/append.rs:249-252`); `contains_event_id` returns `Result<bool, _>` whose `false` conflates *"never had it"* with *"minted it and forgot it"* (`crates/happenstance-core/src/store.rs:268`). Every way to fix that is one of DA-7's four rows and every row is a change to a port published at `0.2.0`. **So the escalation is expected, not exceptional**, and this story's job is to raise it in the form AC-012 specifies — surface, version consequence, option set, which row — and to prove by diff that nothing was changed. That is the outcome DA-3(3) already calls *"the expected one"* (`_decomposition.md:268-270`).
- **Does the project need a `0.3.0`?** **Answered: not from this project, and the finding says so explicitly.** Everything this project adds is additive — new `pub` rule functions in `suite.rs`, at most a defaulted `Fixture` const and method, rustdoc — and ships as `0.2.x`. A `0.3.0` would only be needed to *implement* one of DA-7's rows, which is the escalation's subject and outside this initiative's exit criteria.
- **What if the `0.2.0` baseline is not available when this story runs?** **Deferred to `spec`, with the workaround forbidden.** HS-P0016 owns the baseline and is rank 4 on the same trunk, so it should exist; if it does not, that is a blocker to raise, and **a synthetic stand-in snapshot is not available** (`_decomposition.md:722-727`). `cargo-semver-checks` at the PR grain still answers its own narrower question in the meantime, and the recorded finding must not let the narrow answer stand in for the broad one.
- **DT-7 — one signal or three?** **Answered upstream by HS-S0120 and inherited.** It reaches this story only through the escalation's wording: if the resolution named a three-way vocabulary, the escalation must say that delivering it needs a primitive, so nobody reads the vocabulary as a shipped capability.
- **What form does the escalation take, and to whom?** **Deferred to `spec`.** AC-012 fixes the content — surface, version consequence, options — and `project.md:184-185` fixes the destination: it is raised to the initiative. The artefact's location and shape are the spec stage's call; its content is not.

## Decision

Every other story in this project keeps its additions inside the non-breaking shape by construction; this one proves they did, and states the one thing the tooling cannot. The public-surface comparison against the `0.2.0` registry baseline, together with `cargo-semver-checks` at the PR grain, is run over `happenstance-core`, `happenstance` and `happenstance-testkit`, and the result is recorded in two sentences: no breaking change, with every additive item enumerated and shipping as `0.2.x`; **and** the fact that a new conformance rule is semver-MINOR and can still turn a passing adapter's CI red, which is the mechanism the conformance suite exists to produce rather than a violation of the constraint. Where this project's readers found that loud failure is not expressible in the existing vocabulary, the finding is escalated in the form AC-012 requires — the surface, the version consequence (a `0.3.0` this initiative's exit criteria do not contemplate), and which of DA-7's four rows it is — and no such change is made, proven by a diff that touches no public signature in `crates/happenstance-core/src/store.rs` or `append.rs`. The spec will cover: the two instruments and the different questions each answers; the enumerated additive list; the two-sentence finding; the escalation's content and destination; the confirmation that any `Fixture` addition is defaulted and every existing `Fixture` impl compiles unchanged; and the residual CF-25/CF-26 exposure on the completeness axis recorded **open**, not implied closed.

## The wrong implementation

**`GreenDiffReport` — a finding that says "the surface comparison reports no breaking change" and stops.** It satisfies every existing check, because it is *true*: `cargo-semver-checks` reports additive, the baseline diff reports additive, `cargo xtask ci --fast` is green, AC-011 reads as met. It is wrong because it omits the sentence `crates/happenstance-testkit/Cargo.toml:4-14` writes in the crate's own words — that adding a conformance rule is semver-MINOR and *"can turn a passing adapter's CI red"* — and an adapter author reading the release notes therefore learns "additive" and discovers "red" from CI. This is the specific loss of information the project's own architecture brief flags as *"a nuance that must be recorded rather than discovered"* (`_decomposition.md:77-85`), and it is the same defect one level up that DR-2 names about the pass list: a summary that is accurate and destroys the finding.

Three more, each green:

- **`EscalationAsRecommendation`.** A finding that says a surface change is needed and recommends one, without naming which of DA-7's four rows it is or what the version consequence costs. It reads as decisive and hands the initiative a preference instead of a decision — and it is one review comment away from becoming a licence. AC-012's three required elements exist to make the escalation *actionable without being pre-approved*.
- **`EscalationWithTheChangeAttached`.** The same finding, raised properly, with a small additive method on `EventStore` already in the branch "so the escalation can be evaluated concretely". `cargo-semver-checks` reports additive, every gate is green, and a port published at `0.2.0` has changed — which gate decision 4 forbids outright. The mechanical defence is the diff check at `_decomposition.md:614`: no public signature in `store.rs` or `append.rs` is touched.
- **`SyntheticBaseline`.** A stand-in registry snapshot constructed so the comparison can run before HS-P0016 ships. It produces a number, the number is meaningless, and the meaninglessness is invisible in the recorded finding — the testing brief forbids it by name, and `CONTRIBUTING.md:295-299` already explains why the PR-grain check is not a substitute.

And one of omission, worth naming because this is the last story in the project and the temptation is to close everything: **`AxisReportedClosed`** — a finding that presents the completeness axis as discharged because an instrument now exists. CF-26 says a fixture instrument satisfies **falsifiability and not implementability**, and the portfolio table still asks for *"a device adapter second"* (`spec/SPECIFICATION.md:8003-8032`, `:8090-8098`). The residual exposure is recorded open.

This story adds no conformance rule and registers no mutant; its instruments are `cargo-semver-checks`, the baseline surface diff, and a diff check over two files.
