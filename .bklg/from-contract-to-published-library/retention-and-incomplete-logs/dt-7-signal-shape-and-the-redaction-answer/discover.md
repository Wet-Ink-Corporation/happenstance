## Questions

- **DT-7 — one undifferentiated signal, or transient / benign-permanent / meaningful-permanent?** **This is the story that answers it; it is not deferred, and the answer is owed with its loser named.** The material is now in hand rather than in prospect: the pass list (HS-S0115) says how much the port can distinguish at all, and the reader observations (HS-S0118) say what a reader actually receives. The decisive framing to carry into `spec` is that a *distinction* is only real if it can be *delivered*: the existing vocabulary has no channel for one incompleteness signal, let alone three — `read_decision_model` returns `(Vec<SequencedEvent>, Option<SequencePosition>)` (`crates/happenstance-core/src/store.rs:321-331`), `Guard::is_violated_by` returns `bool` from a two-arm `match` (`crates/happenstance-core/src/append.rs:249-252`), and `contains_event_id`'s `false` already conflates two meanings (`:268`). So a three-way answer inside gate decision 4 can only be a **vocabulary** — words ADR-0028 and the rustdoc use so that a future primitive has names ready — and not a reported signal. Whichever way it lands, the loser is named: the production evidence for three, or the reader complexity that buys.
- **Is "fails loudly" expressible in the existing error vocabulary?** **Answered, and it is DT-7's own cost side.** No — see the three return shapes above. This story must not resolve DT-7 in a way that presupposes a channel that does not exist, which is the specific way this question collides with the constraint here: choosing "three kinds" and then specifying how a store *reports* which kind is ES-39's primitive under a different name, and is an escalation (DA-7, `_decomposition.md:363-379`), not a design decision this story may take.
- **Can a `Tag` be redacted?** **Answered as far as the constraint permits: not by any means available today, and the reachable answer is an explicit deferral naming an experiment.** `Tag` equality *is* string equality with no digest field to swap in (`crates/happenstance-core/src/tag.rs:170-193`), there is no store-side update path, and adding one would amend ES-37, which is `[FROZEN]`. Deciding *to* redact is therefore out of scope by `project.md:111-114`. The deferral must name its experiment, because CF-38 makes an unnamed deferral a gate failure.
- **Which experiment, exactly?** **Deferred to `spec`, with the candidate fixed:** a tag-digest scheme under `experiments/`, measured against `query_item_tags_are_and` and `query_item_tags_match_supersets` — the two rules whose behaviour a change to tag equality would move (`_decomposition.md:462-466`). The spec names the path and what the experiment would measure; it does not run it.
- **Does this story write ADR-0028?** **No — deferred to HS-S0121**, which depends on this one. This story resolves DT-7 in `_design.md` and settles the redaction answer's shape; AC-010 is then *recorded* in ADR-0028 where the AC asks for it (`_storymap.md:96`).

## Decision

Two vocabulary questions sit under this project's answer, and both are decided against code rather than against preference. DT-7 asks how a reader is told the log it is reading is incomplete and why — one undifferentiated signal, or a distinction between transient, benign-permanent and meaningful-permanent — with production evidence on the side of three and reader complexity on the side of one; the decisive new fact is that the port has no channel for even one, so any distinction taken now is a vocabulary that a future primitive could carry, not a signal anything reports today. The redaction question asks whether a `Tag` can be redacted at all, and the code answers it: tag equality is string equality with no digest to swap in, there is no store-side update path, and creating one would amend a `[FROZEN]` clause this project may not touch. The spec will cover: DT-7's resolution written into `_design.md` with the option that lost and the reason, weighed explicitly against the pass list and the two reader observations rather than against intuition; the statement that the chosen shape is a vocabulary and not a reported signal, so that no later reader mistakes it for an answer to ES-39; the redaction answer as an explicit deferral naming a real `experiments/` path and what that experiment would measure, against `query_item_tags_are_and` and `query_item_tags_match_supersets`; and the handoff of AC-010's text to ADR-0028, which HS-S0121 authors.

## The wrong implementation

**`ThreeKindsOfNothing` — a DT-7 resolution that adopts the transient / benign-permanent / meaningful-permanent distinction, writes it into the design and the rustdoc, and never says that nothing in the port can carry it.** It satisfies every existing check, because it is prose: no compiler sees it, no conformance rule tests it, `cargo xtask ci --fast` is green and `spec-trace` has nothing to say about a design artifact. It is wrong in the way this project's whole subject is wrong — it is confidently right-looking and silently undeliverable. An adapter author reads three kinds and reasonably concludes there is somewhere to report them; there is not, and the first person to look for it finds `Result<bool, _>` (`crates/happenstance-core/src/store.rs:268`) and a two-arm `match` (`crates/happenstance-core/src/append.rs:249-252`). Worse, it settles ES-39 by implication — a distinction with a reporting shape *is* the primitive — from a `_design.md` that no ADR authorises, which is exactly the "settled silently in passing" defect `CLAUDE.md` names. Its mirror is equally available: **`OneSignalBecauseItIsFriday`**, a one-signal answer taken because it is cheaper at rank 5 and recorded without naming the production evidence it overrules — the schedule-position risk `project.md:331-335` names by name.

The redaction half has its own, and it is the sharper of the two: **`DeferredToNothing` — "whether a `Tag` can be redacted is deferred"**, full stop. It reads as diligence, it passes review by looking like a decision, and it is a gate failure by construction under CF-38, which is why AC-010 spells out that the deferral must name an experiment. Its close cousin is **`DigestTagsQuietly`** — a resolution that proposes swapping `Tag`'s inner string for a digest so redaction becomes possible. It compiles in the imagination and would pass `cargo-semver-checks` if the type's signature were preserved, because the break is not in the signature at all: `PartialEq`/`Eq`/`Ord`/`Hash` are hand-written and delegate to `as_str()` (`crates/happenstance-core/src/tag.rs:170-193`), so changing what equality *means* silently re-keys every tag index in every adapter — a far larger break than any signature, and invisible to the tool that checks signatures.

None of these is a conformance mutant, and this story registers none: it adds no rule. The nearest executable check is the pair of existing rules a tag-equality change would move — `query_item_tags_are_and` and `query_item_tags_match_supersets` — which is why the named experiment is specified to measure against exactly those.
